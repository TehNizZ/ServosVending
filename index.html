Questions, answered

How does the free trial work?

Every placement starts with a 30-day trial. Whenever a machine grosses more than $1,000 in a month, you receive 10% of that month's sales. If a month comes in under $1,000, Servos keeps the revenue to cover stocking and operating costs. Flat rental options are available too.

Does it cost my venue anything?

No. Servos supplies, installs, insures, stocks and maintains the equipment. You provide the space and a standard power outlet.

Who makes your machines?

Our smart coolers and combo machines come factory-direct from Vending.com and are built in the USA. Phone charging stations are supplied by Boost Barz in Texas, and our trading card kiosks are made by VTM.

Will you stock what my guests actually want?

That's the goal. Our Daytime Mix leans toward cold brew, clean energy drinks, protein bars and sparkling water. Our Nighttime Mix suits bars and late-night venues with recovery packs, mixers, energy drinks and snacks. Smart monitoring helps us adjust as we learn what sells.

How quickly can a machine be installed?

Once you send in a request, our local technicians typically handle placement, setup, testing and stocking within 5 to 7 business days.
